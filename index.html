Which areas do you cover?

LockSora is based in Tooting and serves Balham, Streatham, Wimbledon, Putney, Southfields, Roehampton and Wandsworth. Send your postcode if you are nearby but not listed.

How much does cleaning cost?

Regular cleaning is listed at £22 per hour and deep cleaning at £30 per hour. One-off services have starting prices; the exact quote depends on the property and agreed scope.

Is LockSora insured?

Yes. LockSora Cleaning Company is fully insured for added peace of mind while your home is being cared for.

What does regular cleaning include?

Regular cleaning includes dusting and wiping surfaces, vacuuming and mopping, kitchen surfaces, the bathroom and a general home tidy-up.

New customers can receive 10% off their first clean. Mention the offer when requesting your quote.
